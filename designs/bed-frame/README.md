## The design

64 1/8" wide, 85 1/16" long, 53 1/8" to the post tops.

- **Headboard:** two 2 3/4" square posts with shallow pyramid caps, standing
  3 1/2" above the top rail so they show above the folded quilt. Between them
  are seven wide flat slats, 7 1/4" x 3/4", 1" apart, set 1/4" back from the
  posts' faces. They're flush with the rails' faces, so the quilt lies flat
  and your back rests on a flat panel. The top rail is 1 1/4" x 4 1/2" with
  well-rounded edges (3/8"), so it's soft to lean on and easy on the quilt.
  It's at 49", 25" above the mattress top: shoulder height sitting up.
- **Foot:** no posts. A foot rail 3" above the slats keeps the mattress in
  place, on two short legs whose tops are flush with it.
- **Side rails:** 7/8" x 6", their tops 1" above the slats, on bed-rail
  fasteners so the bed comes apart.
- **Support:** poplar cleats inside the side rails, thirteen 1x4 slats
  about 2 1/2" apart, and a 2x4 center rail on three legs, set in out of
  sight.
- **Wood:** about 27 board feet of quartersawn white oak (buy about 37 with
  waste), 21 of poplar. It's fumed or stained a medium brown in the
  renders.

## Assumptions

- Mattress 60" x 80" x 11" on slats, no box spring; its top 24" from the
  floor. A thicker mattress raises it: 13" puts the top at 26".
- The headboard is mortise and tenon (slats into the rails, rails into the
  posts); the drawing shows the pieces butted, so add the tenons' length
  to the slats and rails when cutting.
- 12/4 oak for the posts (or two 6/4 boards glued up, with quartersawn faces
  outward).

## Questions

- The foot legs are flush with the foot rail's top, so they read as a plain
  corner block, not a post. Would you rather have them set in under the
  rail, out of sight?
- Do you want the headboard panel to sit lower for a taller pillow stack,
  or is 25" above the mattress about right?
