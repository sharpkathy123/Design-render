# Where the bed's fabric textures come from

- `pomegranate.png`, `blockprint.png`, `kilim.png`: our own, drawn by
  `tools/make_fabric.py` (`python3 tools/make_fabric.py designs/bed-frame/textures`).
- `morris-strawberry-thief.jpg`: William Morris, *Strawberry Thief*, designed
  1883 (this piece printed about 1934 by Morris & Co.), from Cooper Hewitt,
  Smithsonian Design Museum, object 18340065. The design is in the public
  domain; Cooper Hewitt releases its images as CC0. Downloaded 2026-11-11
  from Wikimedia Commons: "Textile, Strawberry Thief, designed 1883, printed
  ca. 1934 (CH 18340065).jpg". One repeat was cut from the scan (1560 x 1700
  pixels of the 3840 x 2598 original, found by autocorrelation) so it tiles
  without seams. Morris's printed repeat is roughly 18" across; the model
  maps it at that size.
- `caban_nor_gl_1k.jpg`, `caban_ao_1k.jpg`: the weave and nap of a woolen
  cloth, from Poly Haven's "Caban" (photography colormass, processing Rico
  Cilliers), CC0, https://polyhaven.com/a/caban, downloaded 2026-11-11.
  The sample is 274 mm (10 3/4") square. Only its relief and shading are
  used, under the kilim's own colors, for the folded blanket.
