# Queen bed frame (2026-11-10)

From the client: an Arts & Crafts queen bed frame, very simple, not too much
wood, so that a quilt draped over the headboard (covering all but the posts)
gives a washable designer touch.

Answers (client, 2026-11-10):

1. Wood: quartersawn white oak, for now.
2. Mattress and support: our choice (an 11" mattress on slats, no box
   spring; its top 24" from the floor).
3. Headboard height: what original Arts & Crafts beds used most often
   (about 50" to 54" to the post tops).
4. Comfortable to lean against sitting up in bed.
5. Foot: no posts, just something to keep the mattress in place.
6. Post caps: Arts & Crafts style.
7. The quilt is separate, sized to cover the headboard.
8. The client hopes to build it.

Follow-up (client, 2026-11-10):

- Make it look more like original Arts & Crafts: through-tenons pegged through
  the posts, an arched foot rail, bed bolts with brass covers, a fumed finish.
- Taller, so a tall person can lounge sitting up: the top rail at 56", 32" above
  the mattress top.
- Everything needed to build it, with estimated prices (BUILD.md).
