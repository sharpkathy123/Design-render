# Queen bed frame (draft, 2026-11-10)

From the client: an Arts & Crafts queen bed frame, very simple, not too much
wood, so that a bedspread or quilt draped over the headboard (covering all
but the posts) gives a washable designer touch.

Items marked **(?)** are open questions; the value given is the default
used until they're answered.

## Mattress
- Queen: 60" x 80". Mattress thickness 12" **(?)**; no box spring, the
  mattress on slats **(?)**.
- Top of the mattress about 24" from the floor **(?)**.

## Headboard
- Two square posts, 3" x 3" **(?)**, with a simple Arts & Crafts cap (a
  shallow pyramid or a flat chamfered top), about 50" tall **(?)**.
- Between them, the least wood that still holds the quilt's shape: a top
  rail and a bottom rail, with a few wide flat slats or one plain panel
  **(?)**. The top rail's edges are well rounded so the cloth drapes
  softly and doesn't wear.
- The posts stand 3" to 4" above the top rail, so they show above the
  draped quilt; everything between them is covered.

## Footboard
- None: two short foot posts the height of the mattress top **(?)**, to
  keep the wood down and let the bedspread hang free.

## Side rails and support
- Rails 1" x 6" **(?)**, joined to the posts with knock-down bed-rail
  hardware so the bed comes apart.
- A cleat along each rail's inside, 1x3 slats about 3" apart, and a center
  rail with one or two legs down to the floor (a queen needs center support).

## Wood and finish
- Quartersawn white oak, fumed or stained to a medium brown, as Arts &
  Crafts furniture is **(?)**. A cheaper choice: red oak or poplar for the
  hidden parts (slats, cleats, center rail).

## Drawings and renders
- A drawing with front, side and top views, dimensions and a cut list.
- Renders of the bed bare, and with a quilt draped over the headboard.
